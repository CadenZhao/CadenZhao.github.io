<section id="perturbation-omics" class="editorial-theme">
  <div class="page-shell editorial-theme-grid">
    <div class="theme-identity"><span>01</span><p>Perturbation Omics Technologies</p></div>
    <div class="theme-statement">
      <h2>Make cellular causality measurable.</h2>
      <p>Experimental platforms that pair programmed perturbations with transcriptomic, epigenomic, and spatial phenotypes.</p>
      <ul><li>Perturb-seq</li><li>Perturb-multiome</li><li>Perturb-spatial</li></ul>
      <details><summary>Research rationale</summary><div><p>Single-cell atlases reveal biological heterogeneity but are primarily observational. Perturbation omics moves from association to mechanism by measuring controlled interventions across thousands of individual cells.</p><p>The long-term goal is a unified platform that progresses from pooled discovery to multimodal mechanism and spatial validation.</p></div></details>
    </div>
    <figure class="theme-figure workflow-figure"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/impact-seq-workflow.png' | relative_url }}" alt="IMPACT-seq experimental workflow"><figcaption>IMPACT-seq · In vivo perturbation with transcriptomic and chromatin-accessibility readouts</figcaption></figure>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="cardiovascular-genomics" class="editorial-theme">
  <div class="page-shell editorial-theme-grid">
    <div class="theme-identity"><span>02</span><p>Cardiovascular Functional Genomics</p></div>
    <div class="theme-statement">
      <h2>Resolve the regulatory logic of the heart.</h2>
      <p>Causal maps of the programs that govern cardiac maturation, maladaptive remodeling, and regenerative potential.</p>
      <ul><li>Cardiac maturation</li><li>Heart failure</li><li>Regeneration</li></ul>
      <details><summary>Research rationale</summary><div><p>Cardiovascular development and disease involve coordinated changes across cardiomyocytes, fibroblasts, vascular cells, immune cells, and other populations.</p><p>My future program will connect human genetics, physiological models, multi-omic measurements, and perturbation screens to experimentally testable mechanisms.</p></div></details>
    </div>
    <figure class="theme-figure"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/cardiac-cell-segmentation.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Cell segmentation across a cardiac tissue section"><figcaption>Cardiac tissue cell segmentation · Spatial context, not a perturbation result</figcaption></figure>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="ai-biology" class="editorial-theme">
  <div class="page-shell editorial-theme-grid">
    <div class="theme-identity"><span>03</span><p>AI-enabled Biology</p></div>
    <div class="theme-statement">
      <h2>Let experiments train the model.</h2>
      <p>Interpretable computational systems that predict cellular responses and guide more informative experiments.</p>
      <ul><li>Perturbation prediction</li><li>Computational frameworks</li><li>AI agents for biology</li></ul>
      <details><summary>Research rationale</summary><div><p>Perturbation datasets contain defined interventions and therefore provide an unusually strong foundation for biological prediction.</p><p>Models will be evaluated by whether they identify mechanisms, generalize across contexts, and prioritize experiments that produce new knowledge.</p></div></details>
